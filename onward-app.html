Onward · Maya's evening

Flight 1404 to Rome has just been cancelled.

You're Maya at gate F32 in Paris. Get rebooked, looked after and ready to claim, before the desk queue decides for you.

  1. Tap the alert on the lock screen.
  2. Pick a flight and rebook.
  3. Mark what the desk gave you, or show the agent.
  4. Add a receipt, then open your claim pack.
